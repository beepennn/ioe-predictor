HOW TO USE
  1. Select your Faculty and Semester from the dropdowns above.
  2. Choose a Mode:
    • Predict — Enter your internal assessment and final exam marks to view your predicted grade sheet scores.
    • Reverse — Enter the marks from your published grade sheet to estimate the teacher-assigned marks.
  3. Fill in the Obtained Marks columns. Results update instantly.
  4. Click any Subject Code or Name to view its credit hours, marks structure, and other details.
  5. Optionally enter your Name, Exam Roll No: and College Roll No: for the header.
  6. Use Print or Save as Image or Export PDF to export your grade sheet.
